# Arcade progression

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Revisits are justified by the front
The campaign SHALL justify returning to sectors already cleared: every transition into a revisited sector SHALL carry a front-line call from that sector, and boss arrivals SHALL keep their own boss call.

#### Scenario: Second tour
- **WHEN** the second tour begins its return to Krybor
- **THEN** the player hears a ground team in Krybor calling for air support before the sector's arrival
