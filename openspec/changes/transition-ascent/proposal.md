# Proposal: transition ascent (R3)

## Why
From the operator's second playtest on 2026-11-15 (`plan/operator-feedback-2026-11-15b.md` R3), after "the cinematic
transitions using the fog are such a magnificent improvement!!":
- "During Pursuit the ship accelerates."
- "The background tilts as it slides down, so the ship appears to fly up."
- "The transition radio speaks a front-line call that justifies going back and forth between the same set of levels:
  'Ground support needed in <sector>!' or 'We're getting overwhelmed here!'"

What `cinematic-flow` built:
- The Pursuit lifts the ship's sprite up the screen (`climb`, up to `plan.climb` units).
- It translates the whole world down by a flat 46 units (`tilt`).
- It speeds the flyover with a symmetric `1 + 9 sin(πp)` bump during travel.

The motion reads as the ship moving on the screen, not as the ship climbing through the world. The ground slides down
as a rigid picture, without any change of perspective. The speed peak falls in the middle of travel, so nothing feels
like an acceleration away from the fight.

The slot line (`InterludePlanner.planRadio`) is a seeded category pick (briefing, banter, joke, taunt), so nothing
explains why the squadron keeps returning to Krybor, Ringfall, Ember Moon and the Hollow tour after tour.

## What Changes
- **Acceleration.** The Pursuit gets a monotonic speed ramp:
  - it eases in from the Rout and peaks inside the cloud at the swap;
  - it eases out through the Descent;
  - it replaces the symmetric bump.

  The ship shows the thrust: the plume grows, the hull nudges forward (a 4 % scale-down as it pulls away),
  and fog streaks rush past both sides of the ship. Flyover parallax, ground units and fog layers all take the same
  speed factor, so the whole world agrees.
- **Ascent by camera pitch, not by sprite lift.**
  - The ship rises only a little on screen (≤ 24 units).
  - The world pitches: the horizon line lowers and the ground plane foreshortens as if the camera pitched up. Near
    rows slide down faster than far rows, so the ground keeps its depth while it drops away.
  - A small roll toward the pursuit direction (≤ 3°) sells the banked climb.
  - The cloud hides the seam as today.
- **Front-line calls.** The sector transition's radio slot plays a front-line call from the *destination* sector when
  the run is returning to a sector it has already visited:
  - "Ground support needed in <sector>!";
  - "We're getting overwhelmed here!";
  - and variants (`frontCall`, `frontCall.<sector>`).

  First visits keep today's mission or banter pick. Boss arrivals keep `bossArrival`.
- **Voices.** The lines are specified for recording. Until the voice batch runs (blocked on the audio studio,
  tillandsias#1103), each plays a recorded placeholder clip together with *that clip's own subtitle*, so audio and
  text always agree. This is the existing `placeholder` mechanism. The lines are listed in
  `plan/audio-studio/voice-batch-pending.md`.
- **Reduced motion.** No pitch, no roll and no streaks. The same beats run as crossfades, and the speed ramp is
  capped at ×2.
- **Unchanged:** gameplay, the Rout grace, transition length, skip and pause behaviour.

## Impact
- **Changed:**
  - `lib/game/flow_director.dart`: new `speed`, `pitch` and `roll` beats replacing the `tilt` envelope's role, with
    `climb` reduced;
  - `lib/game/scene.dart`: the world pitch transform in place of the flat tilt translate, fog streaks, and the
    cinematic ship's thrust and nudge;
  - `lib/game/flyover_background.dart`: the speed factor taken from the flow's `speed` beat;
  - `lib/game/atmosphere.dart`: fog layer scroll under the same factor;
  - `lib/game/interlude.dart`: `planRadio` asks for `frontCall.<destination>` then `frontCall` on revisits;
  - `lib/game/model_flow.dart`: the destination sector and the visited set.
- **Data, already in this change:**
  - `art/comms/lines.json`: new triggers `frontCall` (5 lines) and `frontCall.1` to `frontCall.4` (2 lines each),
    all with recorded placeholders;
  - `lib/comms/comms_bank.dart`: regenerated by `tools/comms_pipeline.py dart`;
  - `plan/audio-studio/voice-batch-pending.md`: regenerated with the 13 new lines.
- **Tests:**
  - `test/flow_director_test.dart`: the speed ramp is monotonic to the swap, and pitch and roll are zero with reduced
    motion;
  - `test/interlude_test.dart`: front call on a revisit, mission line on a first visit, `bossArrival` untouched;
  - `test/transition_ascent_review_test.dart`: strips in `art/contact/transition-ascent-*.png`;
  - the comms tests stay green (verified with the regenerated bank).
- **Depends on** `cinematic-flow` (FlowDirector) and `fog-restoration` (cloud layers).
