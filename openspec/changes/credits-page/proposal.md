# Proposal: credits page (a still page, a chill song, everything we build on)

## Why
The operator, 2026-11-17: "do we have a chill soundscore for the credits page? As in, when a user clicks Credits on
the bottom of the landing page? Let's remove the autoscroll, but make it have a nice soundtrack, and display all of
these licenses and models, and the pile of projects on which we build upon. Please include Tillandsias, Fedora, and
Podman, prominently near the top, with icons and links to their websites. A self link to the github repo
https://github.com/8007342/demon_attack"

Today the title's CREDITS opens `EndingPage(creditsOnly: true)`: the credits roll by themselves at 20 px/s (a
ticker, a PAUSE SCROLL button, Space to toggle), the music is the ending's coda (its ukulele, then the beach
reprise), and `assets/credits.json` lists the models, a few production tools and the Dart packages. It does not list
the signed third-party licences, it is missing the Demucs credit that
`openspec/specs/third-party-licence-demucs-weights-unstated` requires, and it names neither Fedora nor Podman.

## What Changes
- **No auto-scroll.** The credits never move by themselves, in the title's credits or after the ending. The reader
  scrolls by touch, wheel, scrollbar or keys (arrows, Page Up/Down, Space, Home, End). Escape closes.
- **Built on, at the top.** Under the creator line: three cards, Tillandsias, Fedora and Podman, each with an icon,
  one line on what it does here, and a link to its website; then a button to the source code at
  https://github.com/8007342/demon_attack and a short not-affiliated note for the trademarks named.
- **Everything else, grouped, with links.** Models used to make the art, music and voices; the third-party licences
  signed in `licences/acknowledgements/`; the CC0 sample libraries of the score's sample bank, marked "coming";
  the open-source projects in three groups (engine and languages; art, music and voice pipelines; planning,
  delivery and hosting); the SDKs and the Dart packages from `flutter pub deps --json`. All generated by
  `tools/generate_credits.lua` into `assets/credits.json` (schema v2).
- **A credits song.** A new song in `art/music/soundtrack/song-map.json`: `credits`, G major, 72 BPM, a 4-bar
  `dawn` that opens into a 16-bar `drift` loop, a slow reprise of the campaign theme. The title's CREDITS cue it
  through `SongDirector` (scene `credits`) on the overture's next bar line, and closing them cues the overture back
  the same way. The music level setting applies as to every song.

## Capabilities
- **New: `credits`** (`specs/credits/spec.md`): the credits page, its built-on cards, its sources, and its song.

## Impact
- Code: `lib/ending/credits_view.dart` (new), `lib/ending/ending_page.dart` (the auto-scroll removed),
  `lib/game/song_director.dart` (the `credits` cue), `lib/main.dart` (one marked line),
  `web/audio/demon-score.js` (the credits song loads right after the overture).
- Generated: `assets/credits.json`, `lib/game/song_map.dart`, `web/audio/score/` (`score.json`, `credits/*.mid`),
  `web/audio/score/mix-trim.json`.
- Tools: `tools/generate_credits.lua`, `tools/score/score_gen.lua` (the `credits` form), `tools/score/songs/credits.lua`.
- Assets: `assets/images/credits/tillandsias.webp` (1.3 KB).
- Not changed: the in-game ending still plays the coda; `openspec/changes/endgame-cinematic` (its "the credits reach
  their end") still holds, now by the reader's scrolling.
