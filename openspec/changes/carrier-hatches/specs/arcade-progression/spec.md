# Arcade progression

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Approach launch waves from the dorsal hatches
The boss level's launch waves SHALL keep their rosters, counts, steps and cap. They SHALL come out of the approaching carrier's upper-tier dorsal hatches, every tier intact, round-robin, projected through the approach distance. They SHALL NOT come out of the prong gates, flank maws, sacs or aft gates.

#### Scenario: Tour 1 approach
- **WHEN** the tour-1 carrier launches its first wave during the approach
- **THEN** each skein leaves one of its four dorsal hatches, alternating sides and tiers, and the gates under the prongs stay shut
