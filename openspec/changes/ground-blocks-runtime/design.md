# Design: ground blocks in the game runtime

Contract: [plan/ground-unit-animation.md](../../../plan/ground-unit-animation.md) (sections below refer to it),
metadata: `lib/game/ground_blocks_atlas.dart` (generated by `tools/ground_blocks`, never edited by hand), render
plan §7 ([plan/render-runs/ground-forces-rework.md](../../../plan/render-runs/ground-forces-rework.md)).

## 1. Presentation only
Every new piece reads `ArcadeModel.ground` and writes nothing back. No tracker, cast choice or animation
phase draws from `model.random` or any simulation stream; per-unit state lives in the painter, keyed by unit
id, and is dropped when the unit leaves. The simulation, its tests and the difficulty calibration are
untouched.

## 2. Loading
`GroundBlocksLayer.sync(model, load, evict)` (called by the scene next to the boss atlas sync) wants the
sector of the current level (`level − 1`, 0–4 → `GroundBlocks.sectors`). It loads the distinct files of
that sector's placement and fx tables (three PNGs) from `assets/images/ground-blocks/`, and evicts the
previous sector's files once the new set is in. A unit whose `sector` differs from the loaded one (a level
change with units still on screen), or a unit with no cast, draws through the old path.

## 3. Cast (`ground_blocks_cast.dart`)
| Game unit | Block unit |
| --- | --- |
| Threat-front vehicle (`vehicle`, not skirmish) | the sector's threat unit: Spout-runner, Scrapmason, Kiln Ox, Belfry (Belfry for the carrier) |
| Skirmish vehicle | the sector's non-threat Host vehicles and walkers, by unit id (Chapterhouse once per list) |
| Heavy tank | Tympanum |
| Demon rifle squad | Rubbling or Ashlar by squad id; member 0 of a squad of 3+ is the Prior, else the Knell-bearer, where placed |
| Demon rocket pair | Corbel Brute, else Ashlar |
| Convoy vehicle | the sector's Fleet roster by the existing variant (Sleet/Cairn, Tether, Lodestar/Sextant, Stilt, Skiff) |
| Allied squads | rifle and rocket troopers |

Only units placed in the sector are cast, so a sector never asks for a block it does not hold.

## 4. Cell selection
`GroundBlockCells` builds names (`unit.layer.state.hNNN[.fN]`) and resolves them through the unit's layout.
State frames that exist at one heading only (special, wreck, arrive, idle, infantry actions) are found by a
search over the authored headings, so the painter never hard-codes a heading or a column. Base cells (hull,
layer, states) fall back to `move` at the shown heading, frame 0, if a state is missing; overlays have no
fallback and draw nothing when missing (the generator drops empty ones). Frame counts are read from the
layout (`frames(unit, state, heading)`).

## 5. Heading tracker (`ground_heading.dart`, §5)
The authored set plus its mirror forms a ring of distinct screen directions in an unwrapped coordinate (Fleet
−90…90; Host 90…270 so 180 sits inside, not on a wrap). Per update:
1. velocity smoothed with `k = 1 − exp(−8·dt)`;
2. target: the velocity angle if `|v| ≥ 4` texels/s, else a facing hint (foe), else hold; outside the arc a
   target clamps to the nearer end;
3. continuous angle moves toward the target at most `ωMax·dt`;
4. when the hold has run out, the shown index moves by **one** ring step if the angle is past the midpoint to
   that neighbour by more than 3°, and the hold restarts (`minHold`).

Class numbers come from `GroundBlocks.turn`. A heavy's 90° swing takes about 1.2 s and shows every heading.
A step reports its direction, which draws skid dust on the outside of the turn (not with reduced motion).

Travel direction: the painter differentiates the unit's screen x (in texels, `x / texel(z)`) and depth z
between paints; the first observation seeds the tracker to the travel direction without a turn (units appear
in the horizon fog). Convoy vehicles never turn round: a travel direction more than 100° from up the plane
is driven in reverse (nose opposite, treads backward).

## 6. Articulated layer (§6)
A second tracker on the layer's angle set with `omegaArt`. Targets: the foe (an ally's attacker; a demon's
ally) from the layer's pivot; a telegraphing threat vehicle aims at the player's ship; otherwise the Fleet
idle scan (outward 22.5° now and then) relative to the hull, and the hull's heading for the Host. The layer is
drawn at `anchor + pivot[h]·s` (x flipped when the hull is mirrored), its own art pivot on that point,
mirrored when its angle is negative, kicked back along its angle on a shot (2 texels Fleet, 1 Host) and lifted
by the walker bob of the stride frame. The muzzle `muzzle[a]` is the friendly tracer's origin.
Composite cells (special, wreck) already contain the layer, so it is not drawn over them; arrivals do not
draw it either.

## 7. States
- **Threat telegraph** (contract unchanged): `fireFlash > ½·fireFlash` → special f2 (launch flash),
  `fireFlash > 0` → f3 (smoking), telegraphing → f0 until 45 %, then f0/f1 at 8 Hz (16 Hz past 80 %), the
  same clock arithmetic as the old columns. `fill1–3` follow telegraph progress. The warning bracket, launch
  flash and muzzle fx are the old ones.
- **Skirmish wind-up:** an engaged Host unit's `fill` stage rises as `tracerLeft` runs below 0.5 s.
- **Damage:** `dmg1` at ≤ 2/3 of the most hp seen, `dmg2` at ≤ 1/3; Fleet turrets add `fx.dent`.
- **Hit:** a white silhouette flash over the hull (allies: `flash`; demons: `lastHit` < 0.12 s), plus Fleet
  armour shards from the fx row.
- **Destroyed Host:** wreck A/B for 2.5 s then rubble cooling A/B, inside the existing fireball, flames and
  smoke; the old tumbling chunks are replaced by the unit's authored debris pieces (quarter turns only). The
  Chapterhouse plays its fall instead of the wreck.
- **Totalled Fleet:** thrown track A/B for 0.5 s, then limp A/B; the turret drawn askew and dimmed; the crew
  pod is the block's pod cell.
- **Pitch:** the relief pose's `climb` taken along the travel direction; beyond ±0.1 the unit shows
  `pitchUp`/`pitchDown` where the heading has one. Ford and crest hop: hooks (`GroundBlocksLayer.fording`,
  `.airborne`) that return false; the simulation has no water or crest events.
- **Arrival:** `arrivalAge` hook (null: no unearthing); the runtime fog and arrival work owns its timing.
- **Infantry:** walk at the tracked heading; idle, aim (A, then B in the last 0.25 s before a shot), fire,
  hit, fall A/B then rubble (Host), run (falling back or totalled), and a flourish for 1.6 s after a saved
  allied squad (every third squad, by id).

## 8. Overlays and level of detail (§6.5)
Overlays are drawn at the same anchor as their base cell, lights and fills glowing through the haze like the
old fire (`_glow`). Depth: z < 900 everything; 900–1200 `lights` only and the layer snapped to a0/a2/a4;
≥ 1200 headings h0/h2/h4 only (5-heading units) and the layer following the hull.

## 9. Feature switch
`GroundPainter.useBlocks` (static, default true). False draws exactly the old path. The old atlases stay
loaded either way: every effect still comes from them.

## 10. Draw cost
Per vehicle: hull + layer + up to four overlays at near LOD, two or three far. One `drawImageRect` per layer
as before (no `drawAtlas` batching; a later choice).

## Deferred
Walker turn steps landing on a stride plant; specials other than the threat telegraph (Cairn deploy, Tether
tow, Reliquary unload, Hodcrawler mend, Carillon ripple, Sextant brace); banners and the Chapterhouse command
pulse; ford, crest hop and unearthing until the simulation exposes them.
