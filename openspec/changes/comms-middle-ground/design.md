# Design

## Decisions

1. **Budget, not cuts, sets the level.** The leaky airtime budget and the jittered rests from comms-pacing stay. Raising `duty` to 0.40 with shorter rests and `burst` 6 lands busy combat at 38–39 % paced airtime, the middle of the 35–45 % target. The duty scales with the music director's `musicIntensity` tier, so a release stretch (tier 1) sits near 24 % and the radio follows the same build-up / peak / release curve as the score. The director listens to the event the music already publishes, and nothing new is coupled.
2. **One rule for the channel: finish, then a grace gap.** `_interrupt` and `_mayCut` are gone. `update` starts nothing while any voice is on air, and every class waits for `speechEnd + grace` (a must-play warning may start one 60 Hz frame early, so it never misses its deadline on a tick boundary). The regroup chorus still overlaps only itself. `interruptedLines` stays, always empty, for diagnostics and tests.
3. **Lookahead from what is already known.** Queued timed calls hold their slots (`_committed`). An untimed line starts only if it, its grace gap and its exchange answer end before every queued timed call (`_clearOfTimed`). The 2.5 s `groundFirePrep` lead alone left 11–16 of ~55 launches with a busy channel. So the observer now reads the model's own launch prediction earlier and publishes it as `groundFireForecast` (fireIn ≤ 5 s, once per unit and shot, read-only). The director reserves the last `fireReserve` (2.1 s) before each forecast launch. The forecast is soft: a launch that slips just finds the channel free. The prep replaces the forecast when it arrives.
4. **When the lookahead still loses.** A warning plans against speech on air and earlier warnings only. Queued dive calls and exchange answers that would overlap it give way: dive calls are dropped, and answers wait. If the line on air ends too late, the warning takes its shortest variant right after the grace gap, ends late, is flagged `late` and counted. It never expires. An urgent sentinel call may also go late, with its shortest variant, if it still starts before the dive. Ordinary dive calls and battle cries are still dropped rather than played late.
5. **Reading time lives on the message.** `CommsMessage.endsAt = max(start + max(duration + hold, readingTime), keepUntil)`, with `readingTime = min(7, 0.9 + 0.055 × characters)` of the text or SIGINT gloss. When an answer starts, its opening line's `keepUntil` becomes the answer's `endsAt`, so both views (side tiles and compact dock) keep the pair up together. The director keeps messages in `active` until `endsAt`, and the views draw `active`.
6. **The dock places cards oldest first.** `CommsHudStack.dockEntries` groups an answer with its opening (two halves) and places entries oldest first while halves are free. Later ones queue in `overflow`, and the newest overflow card shows as a one-line ticker in the chip row. Nothing already on screen moves or disappears early, and the dock never grows past `maxHeight`. The director helps keep overflow rare: flavour waits while an exchange is on screen, and an exchange opens only when the other cards will be gone by its answer.
7. **Ground truth is a small state machine fed by events.** `CommsGroundPicture` notes ground-fight events, the observer's `groundPicture`, `allyUnderAttack`, allied saves and homing kills (the glue passes on homing kills it does not voice). Gates are data: `commsLineGates` by clip id and `commsTriggerGates` by trigger. `_pickLine` filters through them with the event being handled, so flank, force and artillery gates read the event's own data. Without an observer picture (unit tests), "engaged" falls back to a ground-fight event in the last 8 s. "Over the fight" then assumes the convoy is under the ship, which it follows.
8. **Moments reuse the bank.** `comms_moments.dart` wraps the generated maps (`commsHumanBank` / `commsDemonBank`) with `copyWith` retunes and new triggers built from existing clips. The generated `comms_bank.dart` is untouched, so the coming 48 kHz re-render can regenerate it freely. Event aliases (`reinforcementsArrived` → `reinforcements`) and shared cooldowns (`incoming` ↔ `waveStart`) stop one moment that is observed twice from being said twice.
9. **Transition hooks belong to the director.** `hush` holds lines below urgent without muting the voice, so the next-mission briefing waits for the tip instead of being lost. `offer` turns the transition's chosen radio line into an ordinary useful message, so it gets the grace gap and never plays over another voice.

## Risks

- Dive calls for sentinels can now end after the dive starts (2–11 per 10 busy minutes in the sim). They still start before it, and the alternative was cutting a line in progress.
- The fire forecast predicts from the model's reload and approach. With several units waiting on "one tell at a time", a launch can slip and the reserve is spent for nothing. That only costs a little chatter.
- The ticker is one line. Long lines are ellipsized there until they move up into a half of the dock.
