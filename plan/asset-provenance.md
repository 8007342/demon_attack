# Original assets

- `assets/images/combat.png`: original deterministic pixel artwork by `tools/generate_sprites.py` on a 64 × 64 logical grid stored 1× (2048 × 2560). Gargoyle round (2026-11-09): the creatures are original demonic gargoyles posed and rasterised by this project's own 3-D rig `tools/gargoyle_rig.py` (left / neutral / right 3/4 flight frames, molts, flashes, actions, death, elite armour). Hide pixels carry a four-level shading map finished from the project's own ComfyUI keyframes (SDXL base 1.0, openrail++; pixel-art-xl LoRA, creativeml-openrail-m), generated locally with the committed workflow `art/workflows/sprites/keyframe-sdxl-pixel.json`, seeds 650100–650403, prompts in `art/families/sprites/prompts.json` (descriptive gargoyle prompts that name no existing game or character), picks 650101 / 650201 / 650303 / 650400 in `selections.json`. The AI renders contribute texture only; silhouettes, faces, insignia and palette are authored. Molt variants were not regenerated this round (the round-1 Qwen-Image-Edit molt sources of the bird-like demons were retired). No sprite, screenshot or trace of the 1982 game or any other game is used. Superseded: the round-1 bat-demon art (seeds 640100–640403). See `sprite-atlas.md` and `asset-pipeline.md#sprites`.
- `assets/images/ship-weapons.png` / `ship-weapons.json`: original deterministic ship, cannon, muzzle-flash, deploy, shield-bubble and projectile frames by `tools/generate_ship_sprites.py` (64 × 64 grid, 1×). The hull silhouette and palette follow the user-selected research hull `hull-I2-820000-0`; muzzle anchors are copied from `ship-parts.json`.
- `assets/images/boss-carrier.png`: original deterministic carrier forms, mandibles, turrets, damage, blast doors, side hatches and charred wrecks by `tools/generate_boss_sprites.py`, rasterised at its native 368 × 240 per cell.
- `assets/images/sentinel-effects.png`: original deterministic pixel artwork by `tools/generate_sentinel_sprites.py`, authored for this project on a 128 × 64 logical grid per wide cell, stored 1×. Since the gargoyle round (2026-11-09) the sentinels are a horned violet warden and a crowned gold colossus rendered by the same rig as the ordinary creatures (`tools/gargoyle_rig.py`), with eight-pose bat-wing strokes, warning, last-stand charge, tucked rush, stoop-dive and maw discharge frames, armour layers, shield, smoke, flames, sparks and destruction frames. The Atari-era reference informs visual clarity and stepped animation only; no original-game sprite, screenshot or traced outline is incorporated. See `sprite-atlas.md` for its separate atlas contract.
- `assets/images/cosmic-sky.png`: original AI-generated pixel-art starfield, ringed planet, moons, and alien mountain horizon, created with the imagegen tool on 2026-10-20 for this project. Prompt directed a dark navy/teal/lilac/amber arcade backdrop, sparse crisp stars, empty center for readable combat, no text, ships, or demons. Independent Flame celestial layers, stars, tint and horizon motion provide parallax and gradual level transitions.
- `web/audio/midi/*.mid`: thirty-eight original compositions generated by `web/audio/generate-midi.py` from the per-level motifs in `tools/music_motifs.py` (plus the menu's sine-pad motif and the cackle's fallback figure). They are rendered by the browser MIDI-event synthesizer, with no sampled commercial music or external soundfont, and serve as the fallback score.
- `web/audio/music/*.ogg`, `*.m4a`, `manifest.json` (round 2, 2026-11-09): forty-three original cues made for this project. The motifs (riffs, hooks, progressions, fanfare and alien march) are original compositions written as note events in `tools/music_motifs.py`, and none quotes or imitates an existing song or band. `tools/music_guide.py` renders each arrangement to a guide with its own deterministic numpy synthesizer; no samples, loops or soundfonts are used. The recorded cues are AI-generated locally in the project's ComfyUI studio (ComfyUI v0.40.0) with ACE-Step 1.5 from `Comfy-Org/ACE-Step-1.5` at revision `371bb1680f8c88d8c1083f6ffb6330d3d55bff76`, licence apache-2.0. The model files are `acestep_v1.5_xl_turbo_bf16.safetensors` (sha256 `86a1afb0…`), the `qwen_0.6b_ace15` (`fd4590c8…`) and `qwen_4b_ace15` (`ffe5ffb8…`) text encoders, and `ace_1.5_vae` (`6de92e3a…`). Each group has one text-to-music genre reference take (`art/workflows/music/acestep15-cue.json`; descriptive genre/instrument/mood tags only, `[instrumental]` lyrics, fixed seeds). Every shipped level, march and alien cue is then an audio-to-audio re-performance of its own guide (`art/workflows/music/acestep15-a2a.json`, denoise 0.45, with that reference take as `ReferenceTimbreAudio`). The only audio inputs are the project's own guides and its own ACE-Step reference takes, never an existing recording. Seeds, denoise, tags and selections are in `art/workflows/music/cues.json` and `manifest.json`. The accent sprites (`l<n>-accents`) and the `cackle` are synthesized directly by `tools/music_guide.py` (formant synthesis for the laugh) without any model. `menu` is the unchanged round-1 text-to-music loop. `tools/music_pipeline.py` selected, trimmed to exact 120 BPM bar multiples, seamed, normalised and encoded (Opus 80 kbps / AAC 128 kbps) every cue. Each generated candidate has a provenance sidecar (workflow, overrides, inputs); the ephemeral candidate FLACs, guides and reference takes are not committed and regenerate from the spec.

Historical screenshots linked in `research-original.md` are research references, not shipping game assets.

Future combat assets must document their original authoring/generator and logical grid here. Keep deterministic pixel-frame artwork distinct from generated background paintings and concept illustrations. Neither historical references nor a smooth vector approximation can substitute for the production pixel-sprite requirements in `style-guide.md`.

- `assets/images/sector-1.png` through `sector-5.png`: five original AI-generated pixel environments for the frontier outpost, orbital belt, volcanic moons, brood ruins and fortress territory. Generated with imagegen on 2026-10-20; see `sector-style-guide.md` for prompts, palette and animation integration.

- `assets/images/hint-scenes.png`: original AI-generated detailed pixel illustrations, generated and selectively edited with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-22. The prompt requested eight text-free instructional panels in the game's navy/teal/violet/amber palette: four-part heart assembly, carrier hatch targeting, winged-creature dodge, shield impact and replacement, heavy versus rapid shots, area blasts, a giant's committed rush, and shield/weapon combination. A targeted edit replaced continuous weapon beams with short projectiles. No historical sprite or screenshot was used. This 1254×1254 sheet is instructional artwork, separate from the coarse combat atlases. HintArt crops panels explicitly, draws without smoothing, and overlays actual gameplay heart quarters and power glyphs so exact mechanics remain consistent.

- `assets/images/flyover-krybor.png`, `flyover-ringfall.png`, `flyover-ember.png`, `flyover-hollow.png`, `flyover-carrier.png`, `flyover-props.png`, `flyover-creatures.png` and the generated `lib/game/flyover_atlas.dart`: original flyover parallax strips, route landmarks, props and creature frames, generated locally on 2026-11-09 in the project's ComfyUI studio (ComfyUI v0.40.0) with SDXL base 1.0 (openrail++), the pixel-art-xl LoRA (creativeml-openrail-m) and LayerDiffuse transparent conv injection/decoder (creativeml-openrail-m); no hosted service. Terrain strips are img2img from seeded layouts textured with this project's own `sector-N.png` paintings, made seamless by an in-graph roll-and-repaint. Every prompt, negative prompt, seed, strength and raw-output SHA-256 is in `art/families/flyover-runs.json`; the spec is `art/families/flyover-backgrounds.json` and the pipeline `tools/flyover_assets.py` (see [asset-pipeline.md](asset-pipeline.md)). Prompts describe generic original subjects and never name or reference existing games; outputs were palette-quantised to palettes derived from the sector paintings, downscaled to a logical pixel grid and, for creatures, turned into discrete walk/duck/burrow frames procedurally. Review sheet: `art/contact/flyover-contact.png`.
- `assets/images/ship-parts.png` / `ship-parts.json`: **provisional research output, not drawn by the game** (the renderer uses `ship-weapons.png`; the muzzle anchors in `ship-parts.json` are the source of `ship-weapons.json`). These are layered player-ship parts (hull and five weapon modules × fresh/worn/critical) on a 48×48 logical grid at 2×. They were generated locally with Forge `image.generate` at tiers I0 (SDXS-512-0.9) and I1 (SDXL-Lightning-4step), both openrail++, seeds 820000–820050; the current picks are I1 except `spread` (I0), and processed deterministically by `tools/asset_pipeline/pipeline.py` (matte, palette snap, binary alpha, outline, docking, derived wear states). Every part was picked automatically by QA score; no human has accepted any. Full per-image provenance is in `assets/research/asset-pipeline/ship/runs.json` and `pack.json`. These parts must not ship until each one is accepted with `pipeline.py accept`.
