# Original assets

- `assets/images/combat.png`: original deterministic pixel artwork by `tools/generate_sprites.py`, no copied source sprites. See `sprite-atlas.md` for frame coordinates and damage stages.
- `assets/images/sentinel-effects.png`: original deterministic pixel artwork by `tools/generate_sentinel_sprites.py`, authored for this project on a 64 × 32 logical grid per wide cell and enlarged 2×. Contains eight-pose articulated sentinel/heavy wing strokes, separate subtle ordinary/last-stand warning and dive poses, shield/armor, smoke, flames, sparks and destruction frames. The Atari-era reference informs visual clarity and stepped animation only; no original-game sprite, screenshot or traced outline is incorporated. See `sprite-atlas.md` for its separate atlas contract.
- `assets/images/cosmic-sky.png`: original AI-generated pixel-art starfield, ringed planet, moons, and alien mountain horizon, created with the imagegen tool on 2026-10-20 for this project. Prompt directed a dark navy/teal/lilac/amber arcade backdrop, sparse crisp stars, empty center for readable combat, no text, ships, or demons. Independent Flame celestial layers, stars, tint and horizon motion provide parallax and gradual level transitions.
- `web/audio/midi/*.mid`: seventeen original compositions generated by `web/audio/generate-midi.py`, rendered by the browser MIDI-event synthesizer. No sampled commercial music or external soundfont. Since the music overhaul this is the fallback score.
- `web/audio/music/*.ogg`, `*.m4a`, `manifest.json`: seventeen original recorded cues, AI-generated on 2026-11-09 for this project. They were made locally in the project's ComfyUI studio (ComfyUI v0.40.0) with ACE-Step 1.5 from `Comfy-Org/ACE-Step-1.5` at revision `371bb1680f8c88d8c1083f6ffb6330d3d55bff76`, licence apache-2.0. The model files are `acestep_v1.5_xl_turbo_bf16.safetensors` (sha256 `86a1afb0…`), the `qwen_0.6b_ace15` (`fd4590c8…`) and `qwen_4b_ace15` (`ffe5ffb8…`) text encoders, and `ace_1.5_vae` (`6de92e3a…`). No reference audio, sample, existing track or named artist/game/soundtrack was used. Prompts are descriptive genre/instrument/mood tags with `[instrumental]` lyrics. They are committed with their seeds in `art/workflows/music/cues.json`, and the graph is `art/workflows/music/acestep15-cue.json`. `tools/music_pipeline.py` selected one of three to seven fixed-seed candidates per cue (seed, source tempo and cut point recorded in `manifest.json`). It then trimmed the pick to an exact 120 BPM bar multiple, built the loop seam, normalised loudness and encoded Opus 80 kbps / AAC 128 kbps. Each studio output carried a `tillandsias.provenance/v1` sidecar (workflow sha256, model hashes, seed); the ephemeral candidate FLACs are not committed and are regenerated from the spec.

Historical screenshots linked in `research-original.md` are research references, not shipping game assets.

Future combat assets must document their original authoring/generator and logical grid here. Keep deterministic pixel-frame artwork distinct from generated background paintings and concept illustrations. Neither historical references nor a smooth vector approximation can substitute for the production pixel-sprite requirements in `style-guide.md`.

- `assets/images/sector-1.png` through `sector-5.png`: five original AI-generated pixel environments for the frontier outpost, orbital belt, volcanic moons, brood ruins and fortress territory. Generated with imagegen on 2026-10-20; see `sector-style-guide.md` for prompts, palette and animation integration.

- `assets/images/hint-scenes.png`: original AI-generated detailed pixel illustrations, generated and selectively edited with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-22. The prompt requested eight text-free instructional panels in the game's navy/teal/violet/amber palette: four-part heart assembly, carrier hatch targeting, winged-creature dodge, shield impact and replacement, heavy versus rapid shots, area blasts, a giant's committed rush, and shield/weapon combination. A targeted edit replaced continuous weapon beams with short projectiles. No historical sprite or screenshot was used. This 1254×1254 sheet is instructional artwork, separate from the coarse combat atlases. HintArt crops panels explicitly, draws without smoothing, and overlays actual gameplay heart quarters and power glyphs so exact mechanics remain consistent.

- `assets/images/flyover-krybor.png`, `flyover-ringfall.png`, `flyover-ember.png`, `flyover-hollow.png`, `flyover-carrier.png`, `flyover-props.png`, `flyover-creatures.png` and the generated `lib/game/flyover_atlas.dart`: original flyover parallax strips, route landmarks, props and creature frames, generated locally on 2026-11-09 in the project's ComfyUI studio (ComfyUI v0.40.0) with SDXL base 1.0 (openrail++), the pixel-art-xl LoRA (creativeml-openrail-m) and LayerDiffuse transparent conv injection/decoder (creativeml-openrail-m); no hosted service. Terrain strips are img2img from seeded layouts textured with this project's own `sector-N.png` paintings, made seamless by an in-graph roll-and-repaint. Every prompt, negative prompt, seed, strength and raw-output SHA-256 is in `art/families/flyover-runs.json`; the spec is `art/families/flyover-backgrounds.json` and the pipeline `tools/flyover_assets.py` (see [asset-pipeline.md](asset-pipeline.md)). Prompts describe generic original subjects and never name or reference existing games; outputs were palette-quantised to palettes derived from the sector paintings, downscaled to a logical pixel grid and, for creatures, turned into discrete walk/duck/burrow frames procedurally. Review sheet: `art/contact/flyover-contact.png`.
