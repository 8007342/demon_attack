# Original assets

- `assets/images/combat.png`: original deterministic pixel artwork by `tools/generate_sprites.py` on a 64 × 64 logical grid stored 1× (2026-11-09 overhaul), no copied source sprites. Plain body pixels carry a four-level shading map finished from the project's own ComfyUI keyframes (SDXL base 1.0, openrail++; pixel-art-xl LoRA, creativeml-openrail-m; molts via Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 fp8 + Lightning LoRA, apache-2.0); seeds, prompts and selections are in `art/families/sprites/`, provenance JSON is written beside each raw output. See `sprite-atlas.md` and `asset-pipeline.md#sprites`.
- `assets/images/ship-weapons.png` / `ship-weapons.json`: original deterministic ship, cannon, muzzle-flash, deploy, shield-bubble and projectile frames by `tools/generate_ship_sprites.py` (64 × 64 grid, 1×). The hull silhouette and palette follow the user-selected research hull `hull-I2-820000-0`; muzzle anchors are copied from `ship-parts.json`.
- `assets/images/boss-carrier.png`: original deterministic carrier forms, mandibles, turrets, damage, blast doors, side hatches and charred wrecks by `tools/generate_boss_sprites.py`, rasterised at its native 368 × 240 per cell.
- `assets/images/sentinel-effects.png`: original deterministic pixel artwork by `tools/generate_sentinel_sprites.py`, authored for this project on a 128 × 64 logical grid per wide cell, stored 1× (2026-11-09; previously 64 × 32 at 2×). Contains eight-pose articulated sentinel/heavy wing strokes, separate subtle ordinary/last-stand warning and dive poses, shield/armor, smoke, flames, sparks and destruction frames. The Atari-era reference informs visual clarity and stepped animation only; no original-game sprite, screenshot or traced outline is incorporated. See `sprite-atlas.md` for its separate atlas contract.
- `assets/images/cosmic-sky.png`: original AI-generated pixel-art starfield, ringed planet, moons, and alien mountain horizon, created with the imagegen tool on 2026-10-20 for this project. Prompt directed a dark navy/teal/lilac/amber arcade backdrop, sparse crisp stars, empty center for readable combat, no text, ships, or demons. Independent Flame celestial layers, stars, tint and horizon motion provide parallax and gradual level transitions.
- `web/audio/midi/*.mid`: seventeen original compositions generated by `web/audio/generate-midi.py`, rendered by the browser MIDI-event synthesizer. No sampled commercial music or external soundfont.

Historical screenshots linked in `research-original.md` are research references, not shipping game assets.

Future combat assets must document their original authoring/generator and logical grid here. Keep deterministic pixel-frame artwork distinct from generated background paintings and concept illustrations. Neither historical references nor a smooth vector approximation can substitute for the production pixel-sprite requirements in `style-guide.md`.

- `assets/images/sector-1.png` through `sector-5.png`: five original AI-generated pixel environments for the frontier outpost, orbital belt, volcanic moons, brood ruins and fortress territory. Generated with imagegen on 2026-10-20; see `sector-style-guide.md` for prompts, palette and animation integration.

- `assets/images/hint-scenes.png`: original AI-generated detailed pixel illustrations, generated and selectively edited with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-22. The prompt requested eight text-free instructional panels in the game's navy/teal/violet/amber palette: four-part heart assembly, carrier hatch targeting, winged-creature dodge, shield impact and replacement, heavy versus rapid shots, area blasts, a giant's committed rush, and shield/weapon combination. A targeted edit replaced continuous weapon beams with short projectiles. No historical sprite or screenshot was used. This 1254×1254 sheet is instructional artwork, separate from the coarse combat atlases. HintArt crops panels explicitly, draws without smoothing, and overlays actual gameplay heart quarters and power glyphs so exact mechanics remain consistent.

- `assets/images/ship-parts.png` / `ship-parts.json`: **provisional research output, not drawn by the game** (the renderer uses `ship-weapons.png`; the muzzle anchors in `ship-parts.json` are the source of `ship-weapons.json`). These are layered player-ship parts (hull and five weapon modules × fresh/worn/critical) on a 48×48 logical grid at 2×. They were generated locally with Forge `image.generate` at tiers I0 (SDXS-512-0.9) and I1 (SDXL-Lightning-4step), both openrail++, seeds 820000–820050; the current picks are I1 except `spread` (I0), and processed deterministically by `tools/asset_pipeline/pipeline.py` (matte, palette snap, binary alpha, outline, docking, derived wear states). Every part was picked automatically by QA score; no human has accepted any. Full per-image provenance is in `assets/research/asset-pipeline/ship/runs.json` and `pack.json`. These parts must not ship until each one is accepted with `pipeline.py accept`.
