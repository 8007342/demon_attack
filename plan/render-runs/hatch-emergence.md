## 0. How to rebuild (no GPU)

```sh
lua tools/rigkit/bake_poses.lua art/families/hatch-emergence.json --pack --texel 1.2 \
  --atlas assets/images/hatch-emergence.png \
  --dart lib/game/hatch_emergence_atlas.dart HatchEmergenceAtlas
```

It runs the rig per species (hero view, clay, hint and z, clay per pose), fits each committed pick
(`art/sprites/sources/hatch-emergence/hero/<species>-<seed>.png`, registered by the silhouette box), bakes the 12
poses, then finishes (grade, combat ramps, ink) and packs one page with one shared cell (`rigsheet posepack`).
16 s at 8 threads, byte-identical on a rerun (sha256 `bbaf3b24…`). The review sheets:

```sh
flock ~/.cache/tillandsias-project/flutter-test.lock flutter test --concurrency=1 --run-skipped --tags review \
  test/hatch_emergence_review_test.dart --dart-define=HATCH_EMERGENCE_CONTACT=true
magick art/contact/hatch-emergence-fight.png -resize 60% art/contact/hatch-emergence-fight.png
(cd tools/gifstrip && go run . -scale 0.6 -delay 7 -crop 0,40,360,440 \
  "$PWD/../../art/contact/hatch-emergence-fight.gif" "$PWD/../../.cache/hatch-emergence-review/fight")
```

## 1. Library extensions (generic)
- `tools/rigkit/rigkit.py`: named key poses (`articulation.keyposes`: per-slot rigid ops, rotate about a pivot or
  move, applied in order; `articulation.parents`: a slot follows its parent). The tilt grid is unchanged
  (`rig.py art/families/flight-joystick.json --check` still ok).
- `tools/rigsheet`: a family `grade` (gain, lift, saturation) applied to pose finishes before the palette snap;
  `posepack BAKE RIG TEXEL OUT.png OUT.dart|- CLASS HULL...` (hulls × poses, one cell, generated Dart with the rig
  origin inside the cell).
- `tools/rigkit/bake_poses.lua --pack`: every hull with a pick, one page.

## 2. Model (CPU, clay only)
One skeleton, 13 slots (torso, head, arms, forearms, thighs, shins, wings, tail), three species as sets, from the
combat atlas's silhouettes (`plan/sprite-atlas.md`): the violet brute (steel pauldrons, sunk head, short capped horns,
stubby wings), the coral hunter (long jaws, long swept horns, the longest narrow wings, stinger), the gold elder
(crown with a ruby, ram horns, beard, breastplate, orbs, gold finger bones). The rim's centre is the origin, z = 0 the
rim plane, so "inside the hatch" is the half-space below it.

Clay iterations (each a 2 s render of all 12 poses, checked with the rim line and the runtime clip simulated):
1. **v1**: the wings folded "back" pointed up-screen (at a 45° camera, behind is up) and stuck out of the hatch over
   the grip; the glide pitched the body 48° and turned the wings edge-on. Rejected.
2. **v2**: the in-hatch bodies lowered (grip 32–36 units deep); the glide pitched 22–26° with the wings rotated back
   to face the camera. The tucked wings still showed as horizontal spikes.
3. **v3 (current)**: a "tuck" fold (droop along the flank, then swing behind the body) for the grips and peeks; the
   claw-only grip at −29, horns and claws at −25, head and shoulders at −16/−14, the chest out at −9.

hint colours on the green key, cropped round the subject (`/work/tmp/imagegen/hatch-emergence/ctlc-<species>.png`),
with the committed `art/workflows/sprites/carrier-hero-qwen-fast.json` (Qwen-Image-Edit 2511 + Lightning 4-step,
1.6 MP). Prompt: the family's `hero` + `hero_<species>`, negative `negative`. No downloads, one job at a time, the
studio already running and never restarted; no LLM held the GPU (gpu.status: no holders), so nothing to unload.

arms and claws. Rejected: 9010001 (long curved horns past the clay mask, cut by the fit), 9010002 (knee guards read
as extra plates), 9010003 (the darkest paint: after the 12:1 finish the body sank into the membranes).

**Pick coral 9020002.** No cast shadow on the key, the horn tips keep their ivory through the finish, open long jaws.
Rejected: 9020001 and 9020003 (cast shadows on the green key, against the prompt; they matte as a dark smear),
9020000 (runner-up: its thin horns finished as solid ink).

**Pick gold 9030003.** The only seed that reads gold rather than brown after the finish: lighter ochre hide, the crown
and its ruby, the breastplate ruby and both orbs glowing. Rejected: 9030000–02 (brown hide; the elder lost its gold
identity against the coral and violet rows).

**Head-reading guard** (`docs/styleguide/alien-capital-ships.md` §3.4, §3.8). The guard is about the hatch never
reading as a face. Checked per pick on the close-up sheet: in the grip frames only claws (and the violet's capped horn
tips) cross the rim, never a pair of eyes in the dark slot; in the peeks and the held door the eyes sit on a whole
horned head with its shoulders and arm, offset to the held tusk, so the slot never gets "eyes". No pick adds teeth
rows or brow arcs to the hatch. The creature's own eyes and fangs are its species read, as in the combat atlas.

## 7. Head-reading fix (lead review, 2026-11-17)
The lead found that the first draft's hatch close-ups (the strips' rows 2, 4 and 6) read strongly as a **reptile
eye**: an amber almond with a dark vertical slit, inside a round cyan field ring and a round dark vignette. That is
iris, pupil and socket, which the guard forbids (styleguide §3.4, §3.8). It was a runtime-drawing fault, not an art
pick, so no GPU was needed. The redesign (design §1.0–§1.7):
- a ragged, flat-ended slot between two separate tusks;
- light from deep inside the slot;
- an open U field hung from the tusk roots;
- light falling down the hull, and a rim light on the tusks;
- the pulse carried by the rim light;
- a ragged scorch for a sealed hatch.

`test/hatch_eye_guard_test.dart` now scores a hatch at rest against an eye template and a concentricity measure. The
draft scored eye 0.53–0.58 and concentric 0.63–0.67; the redesign scores 0.21–0.22 and 0.36–0.41, against ceilings
of 0.35 and 0.45. The close-up, strip and fight sheets and the GIF were re-rendered.
