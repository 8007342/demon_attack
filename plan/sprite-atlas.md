# Sprite atlas contract (64-grid)

This is the authoritative layout of every combat sprite sheet. The user raised the creature grid from 32 × 32 to **64 × 64 logical pixels** for a 16-bit level of detail: more pixels per creature, the **same on-screen size and hitbox footprint**, still crisp nearest-neighbour pixel art with a bounded palette, hard edges and discrete authored frames. No smooth vector creatures, gradients or continuously bent shapes.

All four sheets are **stored 1×**: one PNG pixel is one logical pixel. Render with `FilterQuality.none` and `isAntiAlias = false`. Coordinates are zero-based `(row, column)`. Lookups live in `lib/game/sprite_atlas.dart` (combat), `lib/game/ship_painter.dart` (ship) and `lib/game/scene.dart` (sentinel, carrier). Every sheet is original: generated by the deterministic tools below, with optional AI-derived shading detail that never changes a silhouette (see [asset-pipeline.md](asset-pipeline.md#sprites)). No sprite, screenshot or trace of the 1982 game or any other game is used.

## Combat atlas: `combat.png`

### Species blocks (rows 0–27)

Species `k` (teal 0, violet 1, coral 2, gold 3) owns rows `7k … 7k+6` (`CombatAtlas.base(k)`):

| Row | Columns 0–7 | Columns 8–15 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| base + 0 | Intact: eight-pose articulated wing stroke | Angry: same stroke, scorched hot membranes, red eyes, open jaw |
| base + 1 | Molt 1 stroke | Molt 1 hit-flash stroke |
| base + 2 | Molt 2 stroke | Molt 2 hit-flash stroke |
| base + 3 | Molt 3 stroke | Molt 3 hit-flash stroke |
| base + 4 | 0–3 anticipation (dive warning): wings cocked high, eyes flaring, a 1 px hunch; 4–7 dive: wings swept back | 8–11 weapon fire (charge, flash, recoil, settle); 12–15 angry weapon fire |
| base + 5 | Intact hit-flash stroke | Death sequence, 8 frames |
| base + 6 | Elite wing-root plates aligned to stroke poses 0–7 (overlay) | 8–11 elite helm/crest/gorget armour at levels 4, 3, 2, 1 (overlay); 12–15 reserved |

**Wing stroke.** Eight poses make one full articulated down/up stroke: raised (0), early down (1), level full span (2), driving (3), bottom (4), recovery with the wrist leading and fingers folded (5), rising with fingers trailing (6), unfolding (7). Humerus, forearm and each of four fingers have their own angle per pose (`STROKE` in the generator), and the membrane panels between fingers are re-rasterised every pose; this is not a rigid wing moved up and down. One stroke lasts **0.5 s** (`CombatAtlas.strokeFps = 16`, so 1/16 s per pose), the same cadence as the old four-frame cycle. Phase is offset per creature by its seed.

**Anatomy and readability.** Head with species horns, slanted dark eye sockets with 2 × 2 glowing eyes, fanged jaw, chest plate, tucked clawed arms and talons; membranes dominate the silhouette. Detail is capped for phone size: no feature is thinner than 1 logical pixel against the ink outline, insignia are at most 7 × 6 px, and every frame keeps a 1 px ink outline plus a top-left rim light.

**Armour, rank insignia and weapon mounts** (per species, visible in every frame):

| Species | Armour | Rank insignia | Weapon mount (lights when it fires) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teal imp | light chest plate | ivory chevron | throat emitter: the jaw opens and glows |
| Violet armoured | steel chest plate, pauldrons, metal-capped horns | twin lilac bars | pauldron barb cannons; muzzles flare |
| Coral predator | pale chest plate | three fang slashes | tail stinger; tip glows |
| Gold elder | crown band, gold chest | ruby star jewel | an orb held in each claw |

Elites add authored overlays instead of code-drawn blocks: gold-rimmed shoulder plates that follow the wing pose (row base+6, columns 0–7), a gold double-chevron rank mark on the brow, side helm plates, crest spikes and a gorget (columns 8–11 for armour level 4 → 1; each hit sheds one level). The dotted rank halo stays a scene effect.

**Damage.** Molts 1–3 progressively: chip and snap horn tips, punch membrane holes and notch trailing edges (more on the left wing than the right), crack the chest, expose a glowing belly, shorten the coral tail and lose its stinger, erode the gold crown, and shift the body to the molt colour. Selection: `molt = maxHp − hp` (clamped 0–3); `hp == 1 && maxHp > 1` uses the angry stroke.

**Hit flash.** Authored highlighted frames (every ramp stepped toward white, outline kept) replace the old full-white tint: intact flash on row base+5, molt flashes in columns 8–15 of the molt rows. Angry and action frames still use a white tint.

**Death sequence** (row base+5, columns 8–15, 0.56 s): flash silhouette, glowing seams, wings shear off and rotate away, a core burst grows, then membrane shreds, bone bits and embers scatter, and smoke puffs dissolve. The scene detects an ordinary demon leaving the simulation with `hp <= 0` (`CreaturePresentation`) and plays this in place of the generic blast at that spot.

## Ship and weapons atlas: `ship-weapons.png`

Every cell is a full layer centred on the ship (logical (32, 32) = ship centre), drawn at 48 × 48 arena units, so 1 logical px = 0.75 units. The hull follows the user-selected research hull (`hull-I2-820000-0`): dark navy delta, long spine, teal panels. Wing cannons are authored **left only** and mirrored for the right wing.

| Row | Content |
| --- | --- |
| 0 hull | 0 hull; 1 hit flash (first 0.25 s of invulnerability); 2–5 twin exhaust frames; 6–9 nose-cannon muzzle flash (single shooter) |
| 1 spread | cyan prism side emitters on the nose flanks |
| 2 rapid | left twin-barrel gold-capacitor wing cannon |
| 3 shield | violet dorsal emitter; 3–5 healthy hex bubble, 6–7 degrading (gold), 8–9 critical (red, gaps flicker); 15 emitter pulse |
| 4 powerful | left long heavy barrel with muzzle brake and coral heat vents |
| 5 large | amber broad shell launchers on both wing roots |
| 6 shots | two frames each: 0–1 ordinary bolt, 2–3 rapid bolt, 4–5 powerful shell, 6–7 large shell, 8–9 spread pellet, 10–11 hostile ordinary, 12–13 sentinel bolt, 14–15 friendly impact |

Module rows 1, 2, 4, 5 share columns: 0–2 wear fresh / worn (scorch pixels) / critical (glowing seams); 3–5 recoil (kick 2 px with breech heat, kick 1 px, settle); 6–9 muzzle flash; 10–13 deploy transition (wireframe sparkle, half-built, metal flash, settled); 14 the spent module centred for the tumbling `FallingPart`; 15 heat overlay flickered over critical wear.

**Firing presentation (no gameplay change).** No wing module: the nose cannon flashes (`model.noseFlash`). A wing module (powerful first, then rapid) fits wing cannons that fire the primary shot alternately left, right, left; only the side that fired plays recoil and muzzle frames, from `model.wingRecoil[side]` over `ArcadeModel.wingRecoilDuration` (0.09 s): recoil column `3/4/5` at > 66 / > 33 / > 0 % remaining, flash frame `⌊(1 − remaining) × 4⌋`. Spread and large modules recoil and flash on every volley. A power collected within the last 0.24 s plays its deploy frames.

**Muzzles are gameplay anchors.** `ship-weapons.json` carries `wing_muzzles` (rapid (−15.5, −10), powerful (−17, −13)) and `nose_muzzle` (0, −10) in arena units, the values the research branch established in `ship-parts.json`. The scene copies them into `model.wingMuzzles` at load exactly as before, so projectiles spawn where they did. The art is built around these points (`generate_ship_sprites.py` `to_px`). Change them only together with `test/wing_cannons_test.dart`.

## Sentinel and effects atlas: `sentinel-effects.png`

Each 128 × 64 cell is the logical grid (the old 64 × 32 at 2× is retired); render at 128 × 64 units, or ×0.5 for compact effects. Rows 0–9 keep their previous meaning so renderer lookups did not change.

| Row | Columns 0–3 | Columns 4–7 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Shield sentinel (violet) eight-pose stroke | ← continued |
| 1 | Armoured sentinel (gold) eight-pose stroke | ← continued |
| 2 | Segmented mint shield envelopes, full → nearly gone | Armour layers: chest plate, gorget, shoulders, outer pauldrons (metal with gold trim) |
| 3 | Pixel smoke plumes | Pixel flames |
| 4 | Breakup / destruction, 8 frames (flash, burst, feathers separate and fall as embers) | ← continued |
| 5 | Sparks | Hull-plate debris |
| 6 / 7 | Standard / armoured last-stand charge (wings raised, crest and shoulder vents flare) | Tucked-wing rush (narrow, coral streak) |
| 8 / 9 | Standard / armoured ordinary dive-warning poses (wings flared, eye band blinks) | Falcon dive poses |
| 10 / 11 | Standard / armoured hit-flash stroke | ← continued |
| 12 | Black smoke columns (critical carrier damage) | Big flames |
| 13 | Shield sentinel beak discharge (charge, flash, recoil, settle) | Armoured sentinel beak discharge |

The wing has area: a covert sheet from shoulder to wrist and six trailing secondaries, plus six separated primary feathers fanning from the wrist. Across the stroke the roots, forearms and primaries articulate independently; recovery folds the span to under 70 % of the full span (58 vs 128 px) and the rush to 38 px. Timing is unchanged: hover poses hold **0.4 s** each (3.2 s per flap); warning and effects run at 8 fps; breakup plays 8 frames over 1.4 s; beak discharge plays over 0.28 s when that sentinel's bolt leaves (hover only). Warnings stay on the creature: no arrows or detached marks. The fairness timings of the last stand (0.9 s tell, 600/540/630 units/s rush) are simulation values and unchanged; rows 6/7 only draw them.

## Carrier atlas: `boss-carrier.png`

Shapes are authored in 184 × 120 design units; polygons, ellipses and lines rasterise at the native 368 × 240 (finer silhouettes, seams and outlines), boxes and dots keep a 2 × 2 footprint.

| Row | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Hull forms: Carrier, Brood Ark, Siege Carrier, Flagship. Each carries the brood sigil (horned crest with ruby) and one rank pip per form |
| 1 | Mandible poses (closed, half, flared, clamped) with dorsal thruster plumes |
| 2 | 0–2 tracking turrets and pulsing core; 3 bay shutters |
| 3 | 0 cracked carapace; 1 exposed rib-core; 2/3 detached wing chunks and shrapnel |
| 4 | Core blast doors at openness 0, ⅓, ⅔, 1: leaves slide into the frame, piston rods extend, hazard chevrons, lock pip when sealed |
| 5 | Left side hatch at openness 0, ½, 1; 3 locked final-form plate with cross-bars (drawn mirrored for the right hatch) |

Damage escalates with hull damage: smoke vents (> 12 %), sparks (> 40 %), flames (> 65 %), then black smoke columns, big flames and shed hull plates (> 80 %).
