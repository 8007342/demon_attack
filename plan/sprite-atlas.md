# Combat sprite atlas

`assets/images/combat.png` is original procedural pixel artwork generated by `python3 tools/generate_sprites.py`. It uses only Python's standard library. No original-game sprite, screenshot, or third-party asset is incorporated.

The transparent RGBA atlas is **512 × 1280 pixels**, eight columns by twenty rows, with **64 × 64 cells**. Each drawing is authored on a logical 32 × 32 grid and enlarged exactly 2×. Coordinates below are zero-based. Render with nearest-neighbor filtering. All sprites face the viewer; the player's nose points upward. The boss silhouettes fit one cell and can be rendered substantially larger.

Demons use horns, scalloped membrane wings, teeth, and eyes to distinguish them from ships. Wing geometry changes per frame. Suggested wing cadence is 0.10–0.14 seconds per frame, played in cell order; player exhaust can run at 0.07 seconds per frame. Explosions should play once over roughly 0.4 seconds. Boss force field, moving door, health indicators, and core vulnerability are gameplay overlays, allowing visual feedback to track actual collision state.

Palette: midnight outline `#100e2c`, teal `#39dfcc`, violet `#a888ff`, coral `#ff786c`, gold `#efbd57`, ivory highlights, and red angry eyes. Background scenery should stay darker and less saturated than these silhouettes.

## Intermediate molt forms

Rows 8–19 append three distinct intermediate damage stages per species. Existing rows 0–7 retain their layout and pixel artwork. Choose `row = 8 + kind * 3 + (molt - 1)`, where kind is teal=0, violet=1, coral=2, gold=3 and molt is 1, 2, or 3. Columns 0–3 are the four wing frames; columns 4–7 are corresponding angry or hit-highlight variants of the same molt.

| Rows | Species | Progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8, 9, 10 | Teal demon | Green/amber exposed body; shortening horns and progressively torn wings |
| 11, 12, 13 | Violet armored demon | Lilac-to-coral body; eroding chest plate, cracks, and torn membranes |
| 14, 15, 16 | Coral predator | Peach-to-gold body; shortened tail, broken horns, and notched wings |
| 17, 18, 19 | Gold elder | Amber-to-rose body; broken asymmetric crown and exposed belly |

All forms keep the animated wing poses. Damage stages change silhouettes through shortened or broken horns, missing membrane sections, crown erosion, and tail loss, alongside visible armor cracks and color progression. A five-hit enemy can display intact, molt 1, molt 2, molt 3, then its original final angry form before destruction. A lower-health enemy can skip intermediate forms. Use actual remaining health to select stages consistently.
