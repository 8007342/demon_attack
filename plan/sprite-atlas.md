# Combat sprite atlas

`assets/images/combat.png` is original procedural pixel artwork generated by `python3 tools/generate_sprites.py`. It uses only Python's standard library. No original-game sprite, screenshot, or third-party asset is incorporated.

The transparent RGBA atlas is **512 × 1280 pixels**, eight columns by twenty rows, with **64 × 64 cells**. Each drawing is authored on a logical 32 × 32 grid and enlarged exactly 2×. Coordinates below are zero-based. Render with nearest-neighbor filtering. All sprites face the viewer; the player's nose points upward. The boss silhouettes fit one cell and can be rendered substantially larger.

| 0 | 4–7 | Gold angry/damaged versions with red eyes and scars, same four wing positions |

Demons use horns, scalloped membrane wings, teeth, and eyes to distinguish them from ships. Wing geometry changes per frame. Suggested wing cadence is 0.10–0.14 seconds per frame, played in cell order; player exhaust can run at 0.07 seconds per frame. Explosions should play once over roughly 0.4 seconds. Boss force field, moving door and core vulnerability are gameplay overlays, allowing visual feedback to track actual collision state.

Palette: midnight outline `#100e2c`, teal `#39dfcc`, violet `#a888ff`, coral `#ff786c`, gold `#efbd57`, ivory highlights, and red angry eyes. Background scenery should stay darker and less saturated than these silhouettes.

## Sentinel and damage-effect atlas

`assets/images/sentinel-effects.png` is a separate original deterministic atlas generated by `python3 tools/generate_sentinel_sprites.py`. Its contract is **1024 × 640 pixels**, eight columns by ten rows, with **128 × 64 cells** authored on a logical **64 × 32** grid and enlarged exactly 2×. Keep the wide cells' aspect ratio when rendering; stretching square cells to make a wide bird would stretch its pixels. Render the sentinel, shield and armor together at 128 × 64 logical scene units; compact effects may use 64 × 32 with transparent margins. Existing `combat.png` coordinates remain unchanged.

| Row | Columns | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0–7 | Broad articulated sentinel wing stroke, eight distinct poses |
| 1 | 0–7 | Heavy armored sentinel wing stroke, eight distinct poses |
| 2 | 0–3, 4–7 | Transparent segmented shield frames; armor layers |
| 3 | 0–3, 4–7 | Pixel smoke frames; pixel flame frames |
| 4 | 0–7 | Distinct sentinel breakup/destruction sequence |
| 5 | 0–3, 4, 5 | Sparks; Powerful pickup; Large pickup (detached warning marks retired) |
| 6 | 0–3, 4–7 | Standard sentinel last-stand raised-wing charge poses; tucked-wing rush poses |
| 7 | 0–3, 4–7 | Heavy sentinel last-stand charge poses; tucked-wing rush poses |
| 8 | 0–3, 4–7 | Standard sentinel ordinary dive-warning poses; falcon-like dive poses |
| 9 | 0–3, 4–7 | Heavy sentinel ordinary dive-warning poses; falcon-like dive poses |

Sentinel wing poses hold for 0.4 seconds each, producing a 3.2-second eight-pose full flap cycle, much slower than ordinary demon frames. Warning poses and smoke/fire effects use eight frames per second; sentinel destruction plays eight frames over 1.4 seconds. Animate the separated wing segments through distinct poses without interpolation or continuous geometry deformation. Use subtle warning poses on the creature itself rather than arrows or detached warning marks. Wing roots, outer segments and tips articulate separately across the eight-pose stroke; avoid merely translating rigid wings up and down. Carrier fire and smoke cycle through the effect frames at stepped cadences; damage changes their count, placement and intensity while keeping the same pixel language. Nearest-neighbor sampling, square logical pixels, a bounded palette and original independently authored shapes are mandatory for future atlas additions. Runtime geometry is acceptable only when rasterized into equivalent hard-edged logical pixels, never smooth vector birds or flame curves.

## Intermediate molt forms

Rows 8–19 append three distinct intermediate damage stages per species. Existing rows 0–7 retain their layout and pixel artwork. Choose `row = 8 + kind * 3 + (molt - 1)`, where kind is teal=0, violet=1, coral=2, gold=3 and molt is 1, 2, or 3. Columns 0–3 are the four wing frames; columns 4–7 are corresponding angry or hit-highlight variants of the same molt.

| Rows | Species | Progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8, 9, 10 | Teal demon | Green/amber exposed body; shortening horns and progressively torn wings |
| 11, 12, 13 | Violet armored demon | Lilac-to-coral body; eroding chest plate, cracks, and torn membranes |
| 14, 15, 16 | Coral predator | Peach-to-gold body; shortened tail, broken horns, and notched wings |
| 17, 18, 19 | Gold elder | Amber-to-rose body; broken asymmetric crown and exposed belly |

All forms keep the animated wing poses. Damage stages change silhouettes through shortened or broken horns, missing membrane sections, crown erosion, and tail loss, alongside visible armor cracks and color progression. A five-hit enemy can display intact, molt 1, molt 2, molt 3, then its original final angry form before destruction. A lower-health enemy can skip intermediate forms. Use actual remaining health to select stages consistently.
