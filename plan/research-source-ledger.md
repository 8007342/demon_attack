# External Research & Sibling-Project Source Disposition Ledger

Research and cross-project review date: 2026-10-26. This ledger records every external academic paper, industry GDC postmortem, shader/steering reference, and sibling Tillandsias forge project consulted for `demon_attack` combat pacing, 2.5D motion/lighting, squadron kinematics, and boss encounter calibration.

Following the provenance discipline established across the forge, each entry separates **what was adopted and how it maps to `demon_attack`** from **what was explicitly excluded or bounded out**, ensuring that external models support arcade clarity and deterministic leaderboard fairness rather than overriding them.

| **Sweetser & Wyeth (2005)**, *GameFlow: A Model for Evaluating Player Enjoyment in Games*, ACM Computers in Entertainment 3(3) (`https://doi.org/10.1145/1077246.1077253`) | **Adopted for challenge-to-skill alignment, control mastery, and immediate feedback**:<br>• Matched early campaign boss challenge to emerging player skill by dialing down Route 0 and Route 1 carrier shield/core HP (`48/16` on Route 0, `64/20` on Route 1 before Easy scaling) and relaxing early boss attack/minion replenishment cadence.<br>• Added responsive player ship acceleration/deceleration inertia, visible banking poses (`shipBank`), and strategic **sling-shooting** (`vx * 0.38` horizontal shot inheritance + up to `+18%` vertical shot speed boost) so skilled pilots can curve shots into carrier apertures while dodging.<br>• Added immediate multi-layer visual feedback on sentinel dive shields (`diveShield`), carrier auxiliary side hatches (`sideDoorLeft`, `sideDoorRight`), and upward dive telegraphs. | **Limits / Excluded**:<br>• Sweetser & Wyeth validated GameFlow qualitatively against real-time strategy titles (*Warcraft III*, *Lords of EverQuest*); it does not prescribe numeric hit-point formulas, frame durations, or arcade shooter projectile speeds. Concrete numbers are verified through `tools/analyze_difficulty.dart` and `tools/profile_difficulty.dart`. |
| **Michael Booth (Valve, GDC 2009)**, *The AI Systems of Left 4 Dead* (`https://cdn.fastly.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2009/ai_systems_of_l4d_mike_booth.pdf`) | **Adopted for alternating Build-Up → Peak → Release pacing and bounded concurrency**:<br>• Separates encounter rhythm from individual enemy durability: finite casualty-triggered reinforcement groups and sentinel escorts create distinct pressure peaks without inflating ordinary demon HP.<br>• Caps simultaneous diving attackers (`maxConcurrentDives = 2 + min(route, 2)`) so high-population swarms do not collapse into unreadable full-screen dive walls.<br>• Preserves explicit inter-wave introductions (1.733 s) and post-sector salvage windows (2.0 s) as earned relaxation phases. | **Excluded — Runtime Hidden Dynamic Difficulty Adjustment (DDA)**:<br>• Valve's adaptive AI Director dynamically alters mob timers and spawns health packs based on hidden runtime player stress metrics.<br>• **Why excluded**: `demon_attack` features a competitive per-mode Top 10 leaderboard (`/api/scores`) with medal, active-time, and remaining-life metadata. Hidden runtime DDA or dynamic heart-drop manipulation would invalidate score comparability across runs; all wave budgets and drop probabilities remain strictly deterministic per mode and route. |
| **Valve *Half-Life 2* & *Portal* Design Research (GDC 2008 / Developer Commentary)**, *Portal Postmortem: Integrating Narrative and Design* (`https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2008/GDC2008_PortalPostMortem.pdf`) | **Adopted for implicit "teaching without text", diegetic telegraphs, and graduated boss weak-point onboarding**:<br>• **Pre-dive upward crest & glint**: Ordinary and elite demons briefly crest upward (`-16 units/s`) with a high-contrast wing-flare glint during `warningTime` before committing to a shielded dive, training players to read motion before trajectory lock.<br>• **Sentinel last-stand tell**: Scarce low-health sentinels execute a 0.9 s climbing raised-wing charge tell before their straight-line sacrifice rush.<br>• **Graduated carrier hatches**: Non-final carrier forms (`phase < formCount - 1`) open both center and Left/Right auxiliary hatches (`±52` arena units, `0.75×` damage) so players learn shield-depletion → aperture-timing with a wider target window; the final form (`phase == formCount - 1`) seals both side hatches and demands center-aperture precision (`sling_shot` hint reinforces curving shots around the hull). | **Excluded — Gated Tutorial Chambers & Scripted Story Stops**:<br>• *Half-Life 2* and *Portal* isolate mechanics in zero-threat puzzle rooms until the player proves mastery.<br>• **Why excluded**: An arcade shooter cannot halt wave flow for mandatory training rooms. Onboarding happens through 4.5 s illustrated arrival cards and graduated in-combat geometry (three-hatch early carrier forms tapering to one center hatch on the final phase). |
| **Mike Ambinder (Valve, GDC 2009)**, *Valve's Approach to Playtesting: The Application of Empiricism* (`https://cdn.fastly.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2009/GDC2009_ValvesApproachToPlaytesting.pdf`) | **Adopted for empirical encounter-budget auditing paired with human play feedback**:<br>• `tools/analyze_difficulty.dart` audits all 318 generated ordinary-wave entrances across Easy/Normal/Hard and Routes 0–4 for total defense budgets, peak active occupancy, and elite birth ceilings.<br>• `tools/profile_difficulty.dart` simulates a deterministic protected pilot to measure wave/boss clear times and verify that Route 0–1 carrier recalibration plus side hatches reduce early boss attrition without trivializing later routes.<br>• Automated metrics are paired directly with human playtest observations (e.g., dialing back early boss difficulty and adding curved squadron entrances). | **Limits / Excluded — Cloud Telemetry & Biometric Arousal Logging**:<br>• Ambinder describes studio eye-tracking, skin-conductance biometrics, and continuous live telemetry pipelines.<br>• **Why excluded**: `demon_attack` is a privacy-preserving browser/Cloudflare Worker game that collects zero player telemetry or analytics outside voluntary 3-character Top 10 score submissions. |
| **Craig Reynolds (SIGGRAPH 1987 & GDC 1999)**, *Flocks, Herds, and Schools: A Distributed Behavioral Model* & *Steering Behaviors For Autonomous Characters* (`https://www.red3d.com/cwr/steer/`) | **Adopted for local neighbor Separation, Alignment/Cohesion, and incoming-shot Evade steering**:<br>• Active hovering demons compute local **Separation** (repelling neighbors within `34` arena units to prevent sprite stacking), **Cohesion/Alignment** (gentle pull toward nearby flockmates within `68` units), and **Evade** (lateral jink away from approaching player projectiles within `dx < 28`, `0 < dy < 95`), bounded to `±22` horizontal and `±10` vertical offsets around their formation anchor. | **Excluded — Unbounded Free-Roaming 2D Boids**:<br>• Pure Reynolds boids roam an open plane using only local velocity matching and boundary reflection.<br>• **Why excluded**: In a fixed-bottom vertical shooter (`shipY = 477` in a `360 × 520` arena), untethered boids either drift out of firing range or collapse en masse onto the player's horizontal rail. Swarms are therefore tethered via a home-band harmonic spring (`homeX`, `homeY`) so local flocking enriches organic life while preserving readable arcade lanes. |

## Explicitly Excluded / Deferred Technologies

| Source / Technology | Disposition in Demon Attack | Why excluded or deferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full Per-Pixel GLSL Normal-Map Deferred Shaders & Screen-Space Volumetric Shafts** (*GPU Gems 3* Ch. 13; custom Flutter `FragmentProgram` normal lighting) | **Excluded / Deferred** | `demon_attack` mandates original Atari/16-bit-inspired crisp logical pixel art rendered with nearest-neighbor sampling (`FilterQuality.none`) on a `360 × 520` logical canvas (`plan/style-guide.md`). Per-pixel smooth normal-map lighting and radial blur shafts soften hard pixel boundaries, obscure small projectile silhouettes on narrow phone viewports, and risk WebGL shader-compilation frame hitches during wave arrivals. Convincing 2.5D depth is achieved deterministically through banked sprite poses, stepped pixel hemisphere/rim highlights, layered atmospheric plasma shields, and multi-plane parallax. |
